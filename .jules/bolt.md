## 2024-05-24 - N+1 Queries in DetailViews
**Learning:** Detail views that pass related objects directly to the template via `context_object_name` (e.g. `classroom.olympians.all`) can cause severe N+1 queries if those related objects access foreign keys (like `olympian.team`). The standard approach of just prefetching in `get_queryset` needs to be enhanced with `Prefetch` objects to apply `select_related` on those deeper relations. Avoid applying these deep prefetches to `UpdateView` or `DeleteView` to prevent over-fetching.
**Action:** When auditing views for N+1 issues, always trace how related managers are used in templates. If deeply nested properties are accessed, use `prefetch_related(Prefetch('related_name', queryset=Model.objects.select_related('nested_fk')))` in the `get_queryset` method of the view rendering that template.
